## What shipped

- **The deductible is recorded on the warranty.**
  - `shop warranty add --deductible-cents N` and `shop warranty update W
    --deductible-cents N`; 0 means none.
  - `warranty list` shows it, or "not recorded". `claim cover` and the
    invoice refuse a deductible that is not recorded, naming the command.
- **On each claim with covered lines** it is applied once, capped at the
  claim's covered work, and split over the claim's lines by amount.
  - The customer's invoice charges it as "Warranty deductible, claim #N:
    labour" and "…: parts" lines, after the supplies line, so the supplies
    percentage does not fall on it.
  - The claim's lines are stored net: what the claim claims for each
    line. The claim's tax is the tax on all the covered work less the
    deductible's.
- **The tax is a reading per jurisdiction and payer,**
  `tax_deductible_rules`, with US-MA basis `reading`, valid until
  2027-10-07:

  | payer | sources |
  |---|---|
  | `other` | LR 79-19 and 85-8 |
  | `maker_with_bike` | LR 03-8 and 85-8 |
  | `shop_contract` | 830 CMR 64H.1.1(5)(g), LR 80-17 and 85-8 |

  - No Massachusetts source names a deductible (`375_sources.md`, with
    its searches and their control).
  - Each row's notes say what is open: 03-8's "no additional
    consideration" condition; whether a deductible is a "separate charge"
    under (5)(g); 80-17's deduction of tax already paid on the parts'
    cost.
  - `shop tax deductible-rule set` records a shop's own reading.
    `confirm` carries the readings forward with their notes, and `status`
    lists them.
  - With no reading on record, an invoice charging a deductible is
    refused.
- **The worked example** (373's case, a 2500 deductible, payer `other`),
  in cents:
  - the customer pays 1630 + 870 + tax 54 = 2554;
  - the claim is 13370 + 7130 + tax 446 = 20946;
  - together 23500, with tax 500, exactly as without a deductible.
  - Under `maker_with_bike` and `shop_contract` the claim is 20500 with
    no tax, and the customer pays 2554.
- **Both export files carry it** with no export code changed:
  - **QuickBooks:** the customer's journal is Dr A/R 25.54; Cr labour
    16.30, parts 8.70, tax 0.54. The plan's journal is Dr A/R 209.46; Cr
    labour 133.70, parts 71.30, tax 4.46.
  - **Xero:** the customer's invoice lines are 16.30 (tax 0) and 8.70
    (tax 0.54); the plan's are 133.70 (tax 0) and 71.30 (tax 4.46).
  - Income and tax equal the no-deductible case.
- **Settlements bill only what the customer has not been charged:**

  - **part approval at 18000:** 2946.

## What 1A and 2A leave out (the operator's addition)

- **A plan whose deductible is per visit,** not per repair. 1A charges it
  once per claim, so two claims on one visit under one warranty are
  charged twice. The Division of Insurance's page says to check which a
  contract does. Modelling it is 1B, a per-claim override, which was not
  chosen.
- **A deductible that includes tax.** 2A charges the deductible before
  tax and adds tax on its taxable share: 2500 + 54. A plan whose $25.00
  is all the customer pays, tax included, would be 2B (2447 + 53). That
  is the plan's term, and 79-19 does not state it. Not modelled.
