## What shipped

The server can now hear that a customer has paid, without Stripe being
able to reach it. The backend stays tailnet-only (the operator's K65), so
the server asks Stripe instead.

- **The payments feed**, `motodiag payments feed run`: about every 20
  seconds it asks Stripe for the eight kinds of payment news the existing
  handler knows, for the platform account and each connected shop, and
  hands each one to that handler (`dispatch_event`, unchanged). Its place
  in the news is kept in the database (migration 089,
  `stripe_event_cursors`), so after a restore from a backup it asks again
  from the older place and applies nothing twice. Stripe keeps 30 days;
  an older place is reported as a gap.
- **The alarm**: every 5 minutes the feed checks in with a Sentry job
  monitor, `motodiag-payments-feed`, with a margin of 10 minutes, so
  Sentry emails after about 15 minutes of silence. The monitor creates
  itself on the first check-in; the email rule is set once by hand.
- **The daily limit check**: one notice each at 50 and at 100 connected
  shops, and at most once a day when news ran more than 2 minutes late or
  Stripe refused asks. What was sent is a row in `stripe_feed_notices`, so
  a restart never sends one twice.
- **`motodiag payments feed status`**, in plain words, exit 0, 1 or 2.
- **`motodiag config show` masks the webhook secret** (F248, closed).
- **The desktop's unit**, `ops/desktop/motodiag-payments.service`, and its
  installer `install_payments_unit.sh`. Not installed yet: that is a step
  below.
- **The guide**, `docs/guide/payments.md`, and the upgrade held as row 401
  (⏸️, until 100 shops or the operator says go), its prompt at
  `docs/prompts/401_payments_news_by_webhook.txt`.

**Which computer:** the home desktop. **Which window:** the Ubuntu

on purpose, waits 20 minutes so Sentry hears nothing, and starts it again.
Leave the window open. While it waits, watch your email: Sentry should
write about a missed check-in after about 15 minutes.

```bash
bash <<'EOF2'
set -u
bad() { echo; echo "SOMETHING IS OFF: $1"; exit 1; }
systemctl is-active -q motodiag-payments.service || bad "the payments feed is not running; do step 3 first"
```
