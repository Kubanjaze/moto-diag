  - They cover work orders, issues, intakes, repair plans, customers,
    vehicles, shops, templates, users, known issues and baselines, plus
    bookings' `updated_at`.

  - analytics' 15 comparisons (F186);

    requisitions, notifications, the priority budget and feedback.
