## What shipped

- **Each settlement is in both files**, against the claim's receivable,
  once per target, in the export whose range holds the shop's day of its
  `settled_at`:
  - **QuickBooks**, a `<claim number>-CR` journal crediting the
    provider's A/R:
    - billed: against the income and the claim's tax inside the
      shortfall, then the shortfall invoice's own journal;
    - absorbed: against the new `absorbed` account.
  - **Xero**, a credit note `<claim number>-CR` to the provider in
    `<out>_credit_notes.csv`. It is tax-inclusive, because Xero's page does
    not give `TaxAmount`'s sign. The billed shortfall invoice goes in the
    invoices file.
- **The worked example** (373's case, claimed 23500, approved 20000):
  - billed: QuickBooks Dr labour 22.34, parts 11.92, tax 0.74, Cr the
    plan's A/R 35.00; then the customer's 35.01;
  - absorbed: Dr Warranty Write-offs 35.00, Cr the plan's A/R 35.00;
  - Xero's credit note: −22.34 and −12.66 (expected tax 0.74).

  Revenue stays as paid in full; tax payable moves only by D11's cent.
- **The tax on an absorbed shortfall stays owed** (2A). It is a reading
  per jurisdiction in `tax_settlement_rules`: for US-MA, TIR 00-3,
  confirmed with `shop tax confirm` and listed by `status`.
  - `shop tax settlement-rule set` records a shop's own reading.
  - An absorbed taxed claim with no reading on record is refused.
- **The CLI:**
  - names each file and its rows;
  - prints the tax each credit note should show (absorbed: 0.00);
  - says how to apply the credit by hand;
  - names the tax reading, or F195 for an absorbed untaxed parts claim.
- **The rest:**

  - a range with settlements only exports;
  - a run with no settlements is byte-identical to master's.

## What the shop does by hand

- **QuickBooks:** apply each `-CR` journal's credit to the claim's journal
  in + Create, Receive payment (Intuit, read through WebFetch).
- **Xero:**
  - import the credit notes file under Sales overview, choosing tax
    inclusive;
  - check each one's tax against the printed line;
  - approve it;
  - allocate it to the provider's invoice named in `Reference`.
- **Before the first absorbed export:** `shop accounting map set --kind
  absorbed`. In QuickBooks, an expense account. In Xero, a code and a
  no-tax rate.
