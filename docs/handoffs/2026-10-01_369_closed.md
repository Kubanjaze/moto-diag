## The cause

`.claude/skills/closeout/_pre_push_guard.py`'s `main()` armed
`signal.alarm(FAST_LIMIT_S + 60)`, 345 s. Its handler, `_out_of_time`,
calls `os._exit(2)`. The cancel sat inside the `try`, so an exception
from `wholetree_gate` skipped it.

Phase 358's
`test_phase358_wholetree_contract.py::TestTheWiring::test_an_error_in_the_whole_tree_gate_blocks`
runs `main()` inside the xdist worker with the gate raising. 345 s later
the worker ended in whatever test it had reached:
- `os._exit` leaves no traceback;
- faulthandler sees no fatal signal;
- the handler's one line went to pytest's captured stderr.

The earlier searches looked for kill calls in `src/` and `tests/`. The
handler lives in `.claude/`.

**The evidence:**
