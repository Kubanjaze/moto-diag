## What shipped

The server side of the library:
- **The upload.** A shop's owner or service writer sends a PDF to
  `POST /v1/shop/{shop_id}/manuals`, tagged with a title, a kind, a make,
  a model and years. The server refuses the file, with the reason, if:
  - it is not a real PDF, or is over 300 MB;
  - it is a duplicate of one of the shop's own files;
  - it would take the shop past its plan's storage (Individual 2 GB, Shop
    20 GB, Company 50 GB, counting the files and not their page images);
  - it is locked, or carries scripts, attachments or launch actions;
  - it fails the virus scan, or there is no scanner on the server (then
    every upload is refused);
  - it cannot be opened, rendered and read.
- **The review.** Nobody in the shop can open a new file until a reviewer
  at Mekno approves it. A reviewer is a new kind of account, granted only
  from the server's command line (`motodiag manuals reviewer grant`). The
  reviewer sees the queue across every shop and opens the pages. They
  approve, fix the tag and approve, reject with a reason the shop sees, or
  take a file down. Any member can flag a page, which sends the file back
  for review.
- **The library.** The server makes each page into an image and a
  thumbnail in the background and indexes its text (FTS5). Members list
  the shop's manuals by bike, open a page at a zoomable size and search
  for words. Only approved files open, and only for the shop that uploaded
  them.
- **The backup and the terms.** The files join the nightly backup. The
  page images do not; `motodiag manuals render` makes them again after a
  restore. The terms and the privacy policy are at version 1.1
  (2026-11-23), with the upload clause and what a person at Mekno sees.
- **The deploy tool** (item 1a, already on master through #88) reads a
  table without a rowid by its key, and so does the census its dry run
  runs.

The seven readings the plan recorded, as built:
1. The reviewer is a table of user ids, not a fifth shop role.
2. The uploading roles are the owner and the service writer (the two with
   `manage_shop`); a tech gets 403.
3. The limit is the shop's plan's; GB is 1024³ bytes; a rejected or
   taken-down file counts until the shop deletes it, and the refusal says
   so.
4. The page images are made again after a restore, not backed up.
5. A duplicate is judged within one shop; another shop's identical file is
   stored again.
6. The terms' upload clause leaves out "(for a company, its own shops)".
7. A publisher's complaint is recorded by the reviewer as a take-down.
