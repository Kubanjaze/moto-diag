|---|---|
| Connect | `motodiag shop payments connect --shop S --email E`, then `shop payments status --shop S` |
| card terminals | `motodiag shop terminal setup --shop S --simulated`, `shop terminal pay INV` |
| invoicing | `motodiag shop invoice pay-link INV` (a Checkout link for the customer), `shop invoice payments INV` |

| set-up check | `motodiag payments check` (prints "set" and "test mode: yes/no", never a value) |

- **An invoice becomes paid only** on Stripe's verified
  `payment_intent.succeeded`, from the shop's own account, for the payment
  this app started, at the exact amount and currency. Starting a payment
  never marks it paid. A repeat, or any order of delivery, leaves the
  same state.
- **F187 closed:** 176's subscription path now changes a tier against
  `2026-09-30.endive`. Subscriptions are re-read from Stripe on every

  - its errors name Stripe and scrub key-shaped text.
