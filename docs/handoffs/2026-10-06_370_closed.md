## What was wrong

`core/session_repo.py` stamped diagnostic sessions with naive local
`datetime.now().isoformat()`, while `_month_start_iso()` returned the UTC
month start. On a month's last evening in a US timezone, after UTC
midnight, a new session counted toward neither month. Reproduced at
2026-10-31 21:42:07 EDT (2026-11-01 01:42:07 UTC), when gate 9's lifecycle
and six Phase 178 quota tests failed (7 of 10).

## What shipped

- **The format:** every session time is written as
  `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS.mmm+00:00` (`core/timestamps.utc_now`), and the
  month start as `YYYY-MM-01T00:00:00.000+00:00`. Why this format:
  - it is the `T` shape the API's `since` and the CLI's filters already
    compare in;
  - the offset is in the string;
  - three fraction digits make it ECMAScript's own format, which the app's
    `new Date()` must parse.
- **The readers:**
  - `diagnose show` and `diagnose list` print local time;
  - `--since` and `--until` are the local day, and a value that is not ISO
    is now a usage error;
  - the report Timeline (PDF and share page) and client memory dates use
    local time;
  - the API's `since` converts any offset to UTC;
  - the predictor reads no session time;
  - the app parses with `new Date()`. No API schema change, and gate 11's
    snapshot did not move.
- **Notes:** a note's in-text stamp is local time with its offset, for
  example `[2026-10-06T15:42-04:00]` (the operator's Q2a).
