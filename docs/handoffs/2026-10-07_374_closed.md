## What shipped

- **How a user checks in with an intake**, `shop appointment check-in
  APPT`:
  - **intake first:** `shop intake create … --mileage N --notes …`, then
    `check-in APPT`. The intake is linked when it is the only open one
    for that shop, customer and bike and was taken within a day of the
    booking; otherwise check-in refuses and lists them. `--intake ID`
    links a named one whenever it was taken;
  - **check-in first:** `check-in APPT [--mileage N] [--problems TEXT]`
    records the intake itself. Without `--problems`, the problems are the
    booking's notes. Without `--mileage`, the mileage is "not recorded",
    and the command prints `shop intake update ID --mileage N` to fill it
    in later;
  - each prints `Intake linked:` or `Intake recorded:` with the intake's
    id, its date on the shop's clock and its mileage. `--wo` links a work
    order as before and prints its intake, or says it has none;
  - a work order's intake still cannot be changed after it is created.
- **The mileage reaching the claim packet:** the packet now prints
  "Mileage at intake" and "Reported at intake", and the coverage verdict
  uses the intake's mileage. In Gate 16's job A (check-in first, then
  `intake update --mileage 31200`), the packet reads:
  - "Mileage at intake: 31,200 mi";
  - "Reported at intake: Front brake squeals and pulls left";
  - a verdict at "31,200 of 40,000 mi".

  With the mileage unknown, it reads "not recorded" and "cannot tell",
  where the bike's 52,000 mi would have read as over the limit.
- **`intake_at`** stays UTC. `create_intake` stamps it from Python's
  clock, in SQLite's format, and the one-day test converts it to the
  shop's clock.
- **Gate 16's inverted test**,
  `test_f189_the_work_order_carries_its_intake_in_either_order`:
  - job A checks in first, and job B takes the intake first;
  - both work orders carry their intake, at the job's mileage and
    problems;
  - no `work-order create` in the walk;
  - check-in printed each intake's date and mileage;
  - the packet carries job A's.
