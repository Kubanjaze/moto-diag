**Self-serve sign-up** means a shop owner, with no help from Mekno, makes their own account, proves their email address, accepts the terms, describes their shop and pays for its plan on Stripe's checkout. Only then do the shop, the owner's login and the owner's membership exist. A user who forgets their password sets a new one from an email.

- an account comes from 330's wizard, with the operator at the keyboard (K125);

- the server sends no email: there is no sender in `src/`;
- `create_checkout_session` (`src/motodiag/api/routes/billing.py`) needs a signed-in user.
