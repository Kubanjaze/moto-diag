THE FIVE SERVER ROUTES (all need the shop tier and an active membership)

- GET  /v1/shop/{shop_id}/payments/account[?refresh=true]
  The shop's Stripe account: stripe_account_id, card_payments_status,
  requirements_due, status_checked_at, livemode, can_take_payments.
  Any member may read it. 404 when the shop has no Stripe account yet.
  refresh=true asks Stripe first.
- POST /v1/shop/{shop_id}/payments/connect
  Body: contact_email (needed the first time), country, currency
  (optional). Answers stripe_account_id, created, onboarding_url,
  expires_at, card_payments_status; 201 when the account was made now,
  200 when it existed and only a fresh link was made. Needs
  manage_billing (the owner). The link is used once, by the owner, opened
  from the app, and never sent by email or text.
- POST /v1/shop/{shop_id}/invoices/{invoice_id}/payments/checkout
  Starts an online payment: answers payment_id, invoice_number,
  amount_cents, currency, channel, checkout_url, paid_when. Needs
  manage_shop (the owner and the service writer).
- POST /v1/shop/{shop_id}/invoices/{invoice_id}/payments/terminal
  Sends the invoice to the shop's card reader: answers payment_id,
  invoice_number, amount_cents, currency, channel, payment_intent_id,
  reader_action_status, paid_when. Needs manage_shop.
- GET  /v1/shop/{shop_id}/invoices/{invoice_id}/payments
  The invoice's payments, oldest first: items of id, channel,
  amount_cents, currency, status (started, failed, succeeded), outcome,
  outcome_reason, failure_message, refunded_cents, started_at,
  settled_at, started_by_user_id; and total. Needs manage_shop.

Refusals are problem documents with a `detail` sentence meant for people:
403 (not allowed at this shop), 404 (no such invoice at this shop), 409
(Stripe cannot be asked yet: no account, onboarding not finished, invoice
not sent or overdue, no reader, no email), 503 (payments not set up on the
server, or Stripe unreachable). Show the server's detail; do not invent
your own reason.

Starting a payment never pays the invoice. It becomes paid only when
Stripe's verified news reaches the server, usually within half a minute.

THE SCREENS (item 7 of the plan)

- src/hooks/useShopPayments.ts: the account read (with refresh), connect,
  the two starts and the payments list, over api.GET and api.POST from the
  openapi-fetch client in src/api/client.ts. 403, 409 and 503 come out as
  typed errors carrying the server's detail, as useWorkOrderParts does and
  as src/hooks/shopAccessErrors.ts and src/screens/shopAccessErrorCopy.ts
  do for shop access.
- PaymentsSetupScreen in ShopStack, titled "Card payments": the account's
  state in words (not connected; onboarding not finished, with how many
  things are still due; ready). "Connect with Stripe" or "Continue
  onboarding" calls connect and opens onboarding_url with Linking.openURL.
  When the app comes back to the front (AppState "active"), read the
  account again with refresh=true, as useTier.ts does for the portal.
- InvoicesScreen and InvoicePaymentScreen in ShopStack: the shop's sent
  and overdue invoices from the existing GET /v1/shop/{shop_id}/invoices
  with status=; an invoice's total; "Send to card reader" and "Pay-link"
  call the two starts; the pay-link goes to the Share sheet
  (react-native-share). The screen then reads the payments list every five
  seconds until the newest payment is succeeded or failed, and shows the
  outcome and the reason, with the sentence that the invoice is paid only
  when Stripe confirms.
- PlanScreen in HomeStack, reached from SettingsScreen: the tier from
  useTier; the three tiers, each with "Choose", which calls
  POST /v1/billing/checkout-session and opens checkout_url; "Manage plan",
  which calls POST /v1/billing/portal-session and opens portal_url. A 402
  from the portal route (no subscription yet) reads "Choose a plan first".
  The existing AppState refetch in useTier flips the Shop tab when the tier
  changes.
