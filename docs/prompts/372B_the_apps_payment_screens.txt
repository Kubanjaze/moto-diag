THE FIVE PAYMENT ROUTES (../moto-diag/src/motodiag/api/routes/payments.py;
all need the Shop tier and the caller's shop access)

- GET  /v1/shop/{shop_id}/payments/account[?refresh=true]
  The shop's Stripe account: stripe_account_id, card_payments_status,
  requirements_due, status_checked_at, livemode, can_take_payments. Any
  member may read it. 404 when the shop has no Stripe account yet.
  refresh=true asks Stripe first.
- POST /v1/shop/{shop_id}/payments/connect
  Body: contact_email (needed the first time), country, currency
  (optional). Answers stripe_account_id, created, onboarding_url,
  expires_at, card_payments_status: 201 when the account was made now, 200
  when it existed and only a fresh link was made. Needs manage_billing
  (the owner). The link is used once, by the owner, opened from the app,
  and never sent by email or text.
- POST /v1/shop/{shop_id}/invoices/{invoice_id}/payments/checkout
  A pay-link (201): payment_id, invoice_number, amount_cents, currency,
  channel, checkout_url, paid_when. Needs manage_shop (the owner and the
  service writer).
- POST /v1/shop/{shop_id}/invoices/{invoice_id}/payments/terminal
  Sends the invoice to the shop's card reader (201): payment_id,
  invoice_number, amount_cents, currency, channel, payment_intent_id,
  reader_action_status, paid_when. Needs manage_shop. No route registers a
  reader yet, so on most shops this answers 409, and the screen shows the
  server's detail.
- GET  /v1/shop/{shop_id}/invoices/{invoice_id}/payments
  The invoice's payments, oldest first: id, channel, amount_cents,
  currency, status (started, failed, succeeded), outcome, outcome_reason,
  failure_message, refunded_cents, started_at, settled_at,
  started_by_user_id; and total. Needs manage_shop.

Refusals are problem documents whose detail sentence is meant for people:
403 (not allowed at this shop), 404 (no such invoice at this shop), 409
(Stripe cannot be asked yet: no account, onboarding not finished, invoice
not sent or overdue, no reader, no email), 503 (payments not set up on the
server, or Stripe unreachable). Show the server's detail; do not invent
your own reason. Starting a payment never pays the invoice: it is paid only
when Stripe's verified news reaches the server, usually within half a
minute.

WHAT TO BUILD (the plan's Logic, items 1 to 4)

1. src/hooks/useShopPayments.ts: the account read (with refresh), connect,
   the two starts and the payments list, over the shared openapi-fetch
   client (src/api/client.ts). 403, 404, 409 and 503 come out as typed
   errors carrying the server's detail, as useWorkOrderParts and
   shopAccessErrors do.
2. PaymentsSetupScreen in ShopStack, titled "Card payments": the account's
   state in words (not connected; onboarding not finished, with how many
   things are still due; ready). "Connect with Stripe" or "Continue
   onboarding" calls connect and opens onboarding_url with Linking.openURL.
   When the app comes back to the front, read the account again with
   refresh=true, as useTier does.
3. InvoicesScreen and InvoicePaymentScreen in ShopStack: the shop's sent
   and overdue invoices; an invoice's total; "Send to card reader" and
   "Pay-link" call the two starts, and the pay-link goes to the share sheet
   (react-native-share). The screen then reads the payments list every five
   seconds until the newest payment is succeeded or failed, or the screen
   loses focus, and shows the outcome and its reason, with the sentence
   that the invoice is paid only when Stripe confirms it.
4. PlanScreen in HomeStack, reached from SettingsScreen: the tier from
   useTier and the three plans by the server's names, each with "Choose",
   which calls POST /v1/billing/checkout-session and opens checkout_url;
   and "Manage plan", which calls POST /v1/billing/portal-session and opens
   portal_url. A 402 subscription-required reads "Choose a plan first."
   useTier's existing re-read updates the Shop tab when the tier changes.
   The app shows no price anywhere: Stripe's own pages show them.
