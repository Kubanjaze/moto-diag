What that means in the build:
- **Stored per jurisdiction:** a shop has a jurisdiction. Rates, and which invoice line types are taxable (labour, parts, diagnostic, shop supplies), are stored per jurisdiction. Each carries its effective date, its source and its stated validity.
- **Massachusetts:** the rate and the taxability come from the Massachusetts Department of Revenue's own publications. Record each with its URL and date; the provenance is `regulation`.
- **Everywhere else:** a shop enters its own rate and taxability, and the same fields and the same check apply.
- **The check:** a rate past its stated validity fails, and an invoice for a shop with no rate on record is refused. Tax is never assumed.
- **Invoices keep their record:** each invoice records the rate it used and that rate's source.
