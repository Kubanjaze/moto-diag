## 2026-09-20 — Plan v1.0

Track M opens, and it opens on an empty subject: not one row in the corpus
mentions Vespa, and nothing at all mentions CVT, variator, roller weights or
MP3. The five Piaggio mentions arrived sideways through Track K's Aprilia and
Moto Guzzi work, and four of them are the Group's dealer tooling — PADS,
DiagCode, the shared parts catalogue. So the tooling is written and the
machines are not, which is exactly the shape of the row.

Two boundaries were set before any research. The generic layers belong to
later rows in this track — CVT diagnostics is 254, scooter electrical 256,
carburettor service 257 — so 251 writes the machines, the way 242-244 wrote
makes and left the generic layer to 246-249. And the MP3's tilting front end
is the row's distinctive subject: nothing in this corpus describes a tilting
three-wheeler, so if the documents support it, it earns a row, and if they do
not, the absence is written.

One thing Phase 250C changed about how this row must be written: the model
vocabulary is now keyed by derived marque, so a new marque's models are
reachable the day its rows land — provided the make column names the marque
plainly. That is a correctness requirement on the authoring now, not a
nicety.

Three sweeps launched with the plan: Vespa's own documentation, the
Piaggio-branded scooters and their engine families, and the MP3 together with
the Piaggio Group USA regulator record. Each fetches every page it cites and
quotes it, or the claim does not exist.
