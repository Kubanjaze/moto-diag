### known_issues rowid 31

**description**

- before: Motorcycle thermostats are wax-pellet valves that regulate coolant flow between the engine and radiator. When the wax pellet fatigues or the spring corrodes, the thermostat either sticks closed (blocking coolant flow to the radiator, causing rapid overheating) or sticks open (allowing constant coolant circulation, preventing the engine from reaching operating temperature). A stuck-closed thermostat is a roadside emergency — the engine will overheat within 5-10 minutes in traffic. A stuck-open thermostat is insidious — the engine runs cold, fuel economy drops 10-15%, oil doesn't reach temperature to boil off moisture and fuel contamination, and cold-running causes accelerated cylinder wear. This affects all liquid-cooled motorcycles: Honda CBR600RR/1000RR, Kawasaki ZX-6R/ZX-10R and Ninja 650, Suzuki GSX-R600/750/1000 and V-Strom 650/1000, Yamaha YZF-R1/R6 and MT-09, and Harley-Davidson liquid-cooled models including Street 750, Pan America, and LiveWire. The thermostat is a $15-30 part that should be replaced every 40,000-50,000 miles or whenever the cooling system is serviced.
