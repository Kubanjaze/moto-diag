## 2026-09-18 — Plan v1.0

The first roadmap row after the 244 series, and it cannot be done as
written. Three primary sources settled it in one sitting: Damon's own
May 2025 investor release puts prototype production in Q1 2026 and says
nothing about deliveries; NHTSA holds no Damon vehicle at all; there is no
owner's or service manual to source from. The row's premise was also wrong —
Shift is ergonomics, not suspension — and the web holds fabricated owner
reports for a bike with no owners, which the refuter lens killed against the
manufacturer's own schedule.

So this is 241's shape: the deliberate absence is the content. The row is
corrected and paused with a trigger; a tripwire pins the absence and the
trap; nothing is authored. The research was run solo through the 242 sweep's
five lenses — no workflow was opted into, and none was needed.
