## S0-4: the per-powertrain rule, and a real step it finds

Items carry no powertrain, so the only thing that can place a
wrong-powertrain step on a walk is its text. Censused every item title
against two vocabularies:

- **engine work**: `engine`, `compression`, `leak-down`, `valve(s)`,
  `carbur…`, `spark plug(s)`, `fuel`, `choke`, `idle`, `exhaust`,
  `emission(s)`, `catalytic`, `evaporative`;
- **traction-battery work**: `traction`, `high-voltage`, `HV`,
  `electric machine`. ("charging" was tried and dropped: its one title
  hit, `ppi_engine_v1` item 2, is the 12-V charging output of an engine.)

Result: 21 title hits. In every template that covers **both** a
powertrain with the work and one without it, the step is **optional**,
the convention Track N's templates use:

- `winterization_v1` 2 (fuel), 3 (carburetor), 4 (engine oil), 6
  (traction battery), all optional;
- `de_winterization_v1` 3 (traction battery), 4 (fuel and engine oil),
  both optional;

**with one exception: `generic_ppi_v1` item 3, "Engine compression
test", is required**, and the template covers electric. Its text:
"Warm engine, remove spark plug, crank and read compression gauge.
Repeat all cylinders." The electric walk prints a required compression
test. This is the planted control the operator asked for (a
wrong-powertrain step on an electric), already live, in migration 007's
starter template.

Beside it, outside the title vocabulary: `generic_ppi_v1` item 4,
"Fluid inspection" (required), says "Check oil color/level, coolant
color/level", which an electric machine without engine oil cannot pass
as written. The rule does not catch it: its title names no engine work.

The same body-text census found engine words in 17 items of
electric-covering templates, most incidental ("cylinder" in master
cylinder, "compression" in fork damping, "exhaust" in cleaning and
covering). The body text cannot carry the rule without a list of
exceptions; the title can.

## Decision

**A test-only gate, as the operator chose; no production code is
needed.** One test file, `tests/test_phase272_gate15.py`, and this
phase's documents.

**The one decision Step 0 forced: the measured wrong-powertrain step.**
The per-powertrain rule is stated as a rule over titles, and today it
finds exactly one violation, `generic_ppi_v1` item 3. Fixing it needs a
migration, which this gate excludes. As Gates 13 and 14 did with every
gap they measured (258's D1, "the gate reports; it does not repair"),
the gate asserts that the violations are **exactly** that one step, so
it fails if another appears and fails again the day it is fixed, and
the step is filed as a finding. It is not a fork: no second plan ships
different code, and the rule, the planted controls and the operator's
three conditions are unchanged by it.
