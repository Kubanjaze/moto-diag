### Migration 086 (schema 85 → 86), one approval (4A)

- **The data module** is `core/migration_086_rows.py`, generated by
  `381_gen086.py` (committed in the phase folder). It compares a copy of
  live with a fresh seed build, by key, and holds:
  - every changed field, as `(row_key, field, old, new)`;
  - the three retired rows, in full.
- **`upgrade_sql`:**
  - one `UPDATE … WHERE row_key = ? AND field = old` per field;
  - the deletes;
  - the checklist edits, keyed by template slug, sequence number and old
    text.
- **`post_apply`** syncs the model and make junctions.
- **`rollback_sql`** reverses each update, re-inserts the three rows and
  reverses the checklist edits.
- **On a fresh database** the updates and deletes match nothing, because
  the seed loads after the migrations. The seed carries the new text, so
  the two paths end equal. That is the parity check, measured by the dry
  run.

### The F158 guard, widened

`scripts/f158_census.py` gains the removed words as patterns: "this
project", "this file", "refut", "corpus", "research pass". A ceiling
keeps their count in content at 0. The ratchet test also reads
`parts.json` and `adapters.json`, the files the database census cannot
see on a seeded build, under the same patterns.

## Refute

Every corrected row is refuted as its own diff before it joins the data
module. The refute skill's rule applies: at most three rounds; rounds 2+
read the diff and its neighbours; after round 3, wording defects go to
one finding, and a factual or citation defect keeps the row from
shipping. Every factual change carries its source, quoted. The checklist
goes in the log's `## Refuter pass`.
