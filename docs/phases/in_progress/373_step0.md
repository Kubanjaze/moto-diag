## S0-2: what the code adds to the picture

1. **Labour is one line.** `generate_invoice_for_wo` writes a single
   labour line, hours × rate, for the whole work order; each part on the
   order (`work_order_parts`, received or installed) is its own line. So a
   claim can cover "the labour line" only whole, unless covered labour is
   recorded in hours and the line is split.
2. **The claim is opened before the work.** Gate 16 opens it at the
   warranty check, before `parts-needs add`: the work order has no parts
   then. Choosing lines when the claim is opened cannot work in the gate's
   own order, so choosing needs its own step after the work.
3. **Prices are fixed in one place**: invoice generation resolves the
   labour rate (`_lookup_labor_rate_cents`, or the `--labor-rate` given)
   and each part's price (override, else `typical_cost_cents`). A claimed
   amount derived anywhere else would need the same resolution twice.
4. **One live invoice per work order** (`_check_existing_invoice`). A
   second customer bill for the same order (a denied claim's shortfall)
   needs that rule changed or a different record.
5. **Payment reads `invoices.total`** (`payments/invoice_payments.py:51`),
   and the export reads the invoice and its lines only
   (`accounting/export.py:157`); QuickBooks refuses an invoice whose lines
   and tax do not equal its total (`:259`). So covered lines kept on the
   customer's invoice, but left out of the total, break the export's
   balance check; covered lines left off the invoice leave the shop's
   revenue for that work out of the books unless the claim is exported.
6. **The API's invoice routes return a dict** (no `response_model`), so
   `InvoiceSummary` is not in the mobile OpenAPI snapshot; the snapshot's
   only invoice schema is `InvoiceGenerateRequest`. Measured in
   `moto-diag-mobile/api-schema/openapi.json`. There is no warranty route
   at all (0 hits for "warrant" in `src/motodiag/api`).
7. **Gate 16's job A is the card job.** If every line is covered, the
   customer owes 0 and the card walk has nothing to charge; the inverted
   gate needs job A to keep at least one uncovered line.
8. **The Gate 16 job's warranty is a "Honda Protection Plan", coverage
   `extended`**: a plan sold separately, which is exactly where the tax
   sources disagree (S0-4).

## S0-4: Massachusetts tax on covered parts (question 3) — a rule question

`373_sources.md` quotes the regulator's own pages, read 2026-10-06. In
short:
- **A maker's warranty included in the bike's price:** no sales tax
  collected on the replaced part (LR 03-8, 2003, quoting a Sales Tax
  Information Letter the DOR calls "no longer published, and many are
  obsolete").
- **A service contract sold separately:** the shop is the consumer of the
  parts and pays the tax when it buys them; it "does not collect the sales
  tax from its customer" (830 CMR 64H.1.1(5)(g), amended 2001 and 2003;
  LR 03-8 note [3]).
- **A maker's plan paying for repairs:** "the entire amount charged by the
  dealer for parts is taxable … whether or not the charges are partially
  or fully covered by the Plan" (LR 79-19, 1979).

**All three agree the customer pays no tax on a covered part.** They do
not settle whether the claim carries tax to the provider (79-19) or the
shop bears it as the consumer ((5)(g)), for a plan the shop did not sell;
and no source maps the four coverage types onto "included in the price"
or "sold independently". The Gate 16 job sits in that gap. Per the
prompt, this is a stop.

## S0-6: decisions taken here, not stops

- **D1. Prices are fixed at invoice generation.** A claim's covered lines
  are recorded as references (labour hours, work-order part rows); the
  amounts, in integer cents, are computed by `generate_invoice_for_wo` with
  the same rate and part prices as the customer's lines, and written to
  the claim then. Before the invoice the packet says the amount is not yet
  derived. Reason: S0-2 item 3, one pricing path.
- **D2. `--claimed-cents` goes.** A typed amount is what F188 names as the
  defect; with live holding 0 claims, removing it changes no row.
- **D3. Covering lines on a work order already invoiced is refused**,
  naming `shop invoice void` and `generate`. Reason: the invoice's total
  is what payment reads; changing lines under an issued invoice would
  change what a customer was already asked to pay.
- **D4. A migration (081) of new tables and columns only**, through the
  deploy skill: live holds 0 claims and 0 invoices, so no existing row
  changes.
- **D5. Not hard-coded to Massachusetts.** Whatever the operator picks for
  Q3 is stored as a jurisdiction rule with its source and provenance, as
  281 stores line-type rules; a jurisdiction with no such rule refuses an
  invoice with covered parts (`TaxNotOnRecord`), as 281 refuses an
  unknown line type.
