  `workflows/models.py` has had `WINTERIZATION`, `DE_WINTERIZATION`,
  `BREAK_IN` and `VALVE_SERVICE` since Phase 114. Only `WINTERIZATION`

**Decision: an extension with no reshape and no new module,** as in 259–261:
one content migration seeding four templates, plus tests. The

because no module is added.

### Row 266: engine break-in

The makers' stages, each with its own measure:
- **Engine speed.** KTM: 690 Enduro 6,000 rpm for the first 1,000 km, then
  7,800 (OM p. 46); 1190 Adventure 6,500 then 10,250 (OM p. 86).
  Yamaha SR400: 3,500 r/min to 1,000 km, 4,200 to 1,600 km (OM p. 39).
  BMW F800R: below 5,000 min⁻¹, no full-load acceleration (RM p. 66).
- **Throttle.** Yamaha XVS95CL: 1/3 to 1,000 km, 1/2 to 1,600 km (OM
  p. 41). Kymco People S: under 1/2 to 300 mi, under 3/4 to 600 mi (OM
  p. 23).
- **Performance.** KTM EXC TPI (a two-stroke): under 70 % for 3 operating
  hours (OM p. 40).
- **Distance only.** Honda CB500F: 300 miles, no full-throttle starts (OM
  p. 14).

First oil change: Yamaha at 1,000 km, oil and filter (XVS95CL OM p. 41).
First service: Kymco at one month or 200 mi (People S OM p. 25); the BMW
running-in check at 500–1,200 km (F800R RM p. 67).

**Rebuilt engine.** Only Genuine's owner's manuals scope a run-in to
installed engine parts: "When your engine is new or when you have
installed new engine components" (Buddy 125 OM p. 25). N3 below finds no
service manual with a break-in after a rebuild.

**Heat cycles.** The word is on no page (N2). The nearest statement is
the Buddy 125's cool-down, and that manual prints two different
schedules: p. 25 (0–100 mi, 10 minutes after every 30) and p. 27 (0–95
mi, 5–10 minutes per hour).

## S0-4: the winterization decision (F158), and is any row not a checklist? No fork

- **264 adds `winterization_v1` and does not extend `generic_winterization_v1`.**
  - The generic template is 007's quick starter, and 259 left
    `generic_ppi_v1` the same way.
  - Extending it would rewrite its four live items.
  - Those items carry figures the library does not support: "winter
    weight (typically 10W-40)" (N6); a "13.2V-13.6V" float voltage, where
    the library has no float-charge voltage at all (its 13.0–13.2 V is a
    fully charged battery's resting voltage at 20 °C; corrected during
    the build, see F161); and
    "run engine 5 minutes" given as universal, where it is Yamaha's step
    with stabilized fuel (XVS95CL OM p. 81).

  They are F159's shape and are filed as a finding, not fixed here: the
  operator scoped this phase's live change to the reference. The
  generic's description loses "Track N phase 264 expands." and points at
  `winterization_v1` by slug, which is 259's option-1 wording.
- **Every row is a checklist.** Row 268's "per-engine-type templates"
  become **one template** (the operator: one template per row) whose
  per-type items are optional, which is 261's drivetrain shape. Inline-four,
  boxer and desmodromic engines share one item that says the library
  holds no figure for them (N1, N4, N5).
