## S0-5 — what this session cannot do, and what it does instead

- **No Mac Terminal, no live upload.** The thread runs in the cloud; the
  upload, the www redirect and the read-back are the operator's, by hand,
  in the handoff's steps.
- **Hooks.** The edit guard and the push guard are `PreToolUse` hooks of a
  session started at the repository root. This thread's session was not
  started there, so they may not fire. The thread runs `wholetree.sh` on its
  own before every commit and reads its exit code, as rule 3 and rule 6 say,
  and pushes with plain `git push`.
- **The founder block's name, line and photo** come from the operator in
  this thread (K110), never from anywhere else.
