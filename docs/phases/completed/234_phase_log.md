### 2026-09-08 — Paired research, close

- **Written from the paired run with Phase 233**, launching no research
  of its own. `radial valve` returned **zero files** corpus-wide before
  this phase.
- **A refuter caught the research contaminating this phase from its
  sibling.** The swing-arm screw campaign was attached to the F4; the
  regulator scopes it to three-cylinder models only and an F4 query
  returns nothing. The finding had carried its own warning against
  exactly that and then did it. The campaign lives in 233's file, and
  tests on **both** sides assert the boundary.
- **The row's framing was replaced rather than repeated.** "Exotic
  pricing, limited dealer network" is an impression. The specifics: the
  workshop manual exists and names its special tools, so mechanical work
  is documented and an independent with the tools can do it; what
  constrains is parts, tooling with no generic substitute, and campaign
  status — including a front brake master cylinder campaign that makes a
  braking complaint a frame-number question first.
- **The shim diameter is deliberately absent and that absence is the
  entry.** Sources disagreed, none authoritative. The file says measure
  an existing shim before ordering, because the default is to reach for
  the Japanese kit on the shelf and the mismatch is otherwise found with
  the camshafts already out.
- **"Radial valve" demystified**: it describes valve placement, the cam
  lobes are ground at a compensating angle onto conventional bucket
  tappets, and the manufacturer's manual describes an ordinary
  shim-under-bucket job. An online shim-over-bucket claim contradicts
  that manual and the file names it in order to correct it.
- **Model numbers are wrong in both directions** — 1078 is genuine, 990
  is a 998, 989 is a 982, and two differently badged machines share one
  engine. The worst trap is **two different 998cc engines** with
  different bore and stroke, so the nominal capacity does not identify a
  piston. One circulating figure is arithmetically impossible for its own
  stated bore and stroke; it is described rather than printed.
- **Two retitles were needed** because the designation bar requires the
  make in full in the title, and "MV four" does not satisfy it. Caught by
  the guard rather than in review.
