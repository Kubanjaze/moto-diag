## S0-2: what the code adds

1. **A claim's covered lines are stored whole.** `warranty_claim_lines`
   holds the hours or quantity and `amount_cents`. A deductible taken off
   needs its share per line, or the export's per-kind credits and Xero's
   per-line tax cannot be formed.
2. **The customer's lines are formed from what no claim covers.** A
   deductible adds lines to the customer's invoice. The tax on them comes
   from the same `decision.taxable_types`, so a deductible split by kind
   is taxed by kind with no new tax arithmetic.
3. **The shortfall invoice reads `covered_cents` and the line amounts.**
   A denial bills `covered_cents` whole, so with a deductible already
   charged, the customer would pay it twice (worked below).
4. **Regenerating an invoice re-derives the claim's amount.** A claim
   past draft whose amount would change is refused (`:395`). A deductible
   derived the same way keeps the same amount. A changed deductible on
   the warranty is refused for a claim past draft, by the check that
   exists.
5. **Gate 16's warranty** has a payer and provider and no deductible.

## S0-3: Step 0's verbs

- record a deductible: `shop warranty add --deductible-cents N`,
  `shop warranty update W --deductible-cents N`;
- see it: `warranty list`, `claim show` and `claim packet`, and the
  invoice's lines and notes;
- charge it, and take it off the claim: `shop invoice generate`, with
  nothing typed;
- the tax reading: `shop tax deductible-rule set`, `confirm`, `status`;
- the export: `shop accounting export`;
- settle: `shop warranty claim settle`.

## S0-4: the questions

**The worked example, in cents.** It is 373's case: covered labour 15000,
covered parts 8000, and the claim's tax 500 (6.25 % of the parts; payer
`other`). Without a deductible the claim is 23500. The deductible is 2500.

### Q1. Where the deductible is recorded, and how much applies

- **1A: on the warranty only, once per claim (recommended).**
  - `warranties.deductible_cents`: NULL is not recorded, 0 is none.
  - Every claim that covers lines applies it once: 79-19's "for any
    single covered repair".
  - Covering lines is refused while the warranty's deductible is NULL,
    naming `warranty update W --deductible-cents N` (0 for none). The
    same rule already applies to the payer and the provider. Nothing is
    assumed to be zero.
  - **More than the covered work:** the deductible applied is capped at
    the claim's covered work before tax. The customer pays the whole
    covered work as the deductible, and the claim's amount is 0. The
    export already leaves a 0 claim out (`amount_claimed_cents > 0`).
    The packet and the invoice's note say it was capped.
  - Example: the deductible applied is 2500. With 2000 covered, it would
    be 2000, and the claim 0.
- **1B: 1A plus a per-claim override.** `claim cover --deductible-cents
  N` would cover a plan that charges "in most instances" (79-19) or once
  per visit (D6: "for each service call or for each repair"), as when two
  claims on one visit are charged once. The cost is a typed amount per
  claim, the kind of typed amount F188 removed.
- **Not offered: a deductible per work order.** A visit's two claims may
  be under two warranties.

### Q2. The tax on a deductible, by payer

No Massachusetts source rules on a warranty deductible by name. The
searches and the positive control are in `375_sources.md`. What the
sources do settle:
- **`other` (79-19, ruling 3):** the entire covered parts charge is
  taxable, "partially or fully covered". A deductible changes who pays,
  not the tax: the total stays 500.
- **`maker_with_bike` (03-8):** no tax is stated only for a repair "with
  no additional consideration from the retail customer". A deductible is
  consideration in money, so leaving its parts share untaxed has no
  source.
- **`shop_contract` ((5)(g), 80-17):** tax is collected on a "separate
  charge" for property the contract price does not include. Whether a
  deductible is one is not settled.
- **Every payer (85-8):** a deductible charged as one line, without its
  parts share stated, is taxable whole.

The deductible is split over the claim's covered lines by amount (largest
remainder, 373's `_split_cents`): labour 1630, parts 870.

- **2A: the deductible's taxable share is taxed to the customer, for
  every payer; the claim keeps the rest (recommended).**
  - The customer's deductible lines are taxed by the shop's line rules
    on the day, as any line is: 870 × 6.25 % = 54.375, rounded to 54.
    The customer pays 2554.
  - `other`: the claim's tax is the tax on all covered parts less the
    customer's, 500 − 54 = 446, so the total is exactly 79-19's 500. The
    claim's lines are 13370 + 7130 = 20500, and it claims 20946.
    Together, 2554 + 20946 = 23500, the same as without a deductible.
  - `maker_with_bike` and `shop_contract`: the claim carries no tax, as
    today, and claims 20500. The customer pays 54 on the deductible's
    parts share. That is 54 more tax than nothing, because the sources
    give no basis for 0 (03-8's condition; (5)(g)'s "separate charge").
  - Recorded per jurisdiction as a reading, in a new
    `tax_deductible_rules` table, with one row per payer. Each row has
    `deductible_tax` = `taxable_share` (the only value allowed, as 376
    allowed only `stays_owed`), basis `reading`, valid 12 months, and its
    source:

    | US-MA payer | source |
    |---|---|
    | `other` | 79-19 and 85-8 |
    | `maker_with_bike` | 03-8 and 85-8 |
    | `shop_contract` | (5)(g), 80-17 and 85-8 |

    Each row is for the shop's accountant to confirm, and is re-checked
    by `shop tax confirm`. A jurisdiction with no row for the payer
    refuses the invoice, naming `shop tax deductible-rule set`. The
    invoice goes ahead without a row only when the deductible is 0.
- **2B: as 2A, with the deductible tax-inclusive.** The customer pays
  exactly 2500: before tax 2447 (labour 1596, parts 851), tax 53. Under
  `other` the claim is 20553 + 447. The totals are the same, 500 and
  23500. Whether "$25.00" includes tax is a plan term that 79-19 does not
  state. It would be the only tax-inclusive line in the system, and its
  before-tax amount would be worked backwards.
- **2C: the deductible untaxed, and the claim keeps 373's tax.** Under
  `other` the total is still 500, but the claim's 500 sits on 7130 of
  parts: 7.01 %, a rate Xero's line shows and an accountant would
  question. Under `maker_with_bike` and `shop_contract` it **lowers** the
  tax to 0 against 03-8's condition and (5)(g)'s separate charge, with no
  source. **Not recommended. It is offered only so you can rule it out.**

### Q3. How it books (2A's figures)

- **3A: the deductible as the customer's lines of the covered kinds
  (recommended).** One line per covered kind on the customer's invoice:
  "Warranty deductible, claim #N: labour" 1630 (`labor`), and "…: parts"
  870 (`parts`). They take the shop's existing income accounts and tax
  rules. No new account and no new mapping.
  - Each covered claim line keeps its share (`deductible_cents`). The
    claim stores `deductible_cents` 2500 and credits each kind net of
    it.
  - **QuickBooks:**
    - the customer's journal: Dr A/R (customer) 25.54; Cr labour
      16.30, parts 8.70, sales tax 0.54;
    - the claim's journal: Dr A/R (plan) 209.46; Cr labour 133.70,
      parts 71.30, sales tax 4.46.
    - Income by kind is 150.00 and 80.00, and tax 5.00, exactly as
      without a deductible. Only the receivable is split, 25.54 to the
      customer and 209.46 to the plan.
  - **Xero:**
    - the customer's invoice: labour 16.30 with tax 0, parts 8.70 with
      tax 0.54;
    - the plan's invoice: labour 133.70 with tax 0, parts 71.30 with
      tax 4.46.
  - The claim's balance check (`export.py:554`) becomes: lines less
    their deductible, plus tax, equals the amount claimed.
- **3B: a new `deductible` account kind, one line.** It cannot carry a
  parts-only tax. Xero taxes a line at one rate, so the line would need
  a split anyway, and under 85-8 an unsplit deductible is taxable whole
  (2500 × 6.25 % = 156). It also rebuilds `accounting_accounts` again
  for its CHECK. Not recommended.

### Q4. A deductible and a settlement

With 2A and 3A, the claim's figures are net of the deductible: 20500
before tax, tax 446, claimed 20946.

- **4A: settle against the claim net of the deductible (recommended).**
  The shortfall invoice reads each claim line's amount less its
  deductible. Its pre-tax total is the net covered work (20500), not the
  gross (23000).
  - **Denial, shortfall 20946.** The customer is billed 13370 + 7130,
    with tax 446 on the day, owing 20946. Their total is 2554 + 20946 =
    23500, what the repair costs with no warranty. The deductible is
    charged once.
    - QuickBooks, billed: `-CR`: Dr labour 133.70, parts 71.30, tax
      4.46; Cr A/R (plan) 209.46. Then the shortfall invoice: Dr A/R
      (customer) 209.46; Cr 133.70, 71.30, 4.46.
    - Absorbed: Dr Warranty Write-offs 209.46; Cr A/R (plan) 209.46.
    - Xero credit note: labour −133.70; parts −(71.30 + 4.46) = −75.76,
      expected tax 4.46. Absorbed: one line −209.46, expected tax 0.00.
  - **Part approval at 18000, shortfall 2946.** Before tax, 20500 × 2946
    / 20946 = 2883.27, rounded to 2883: labour 1880, parts 1003. The
    customer's tax is 1003 × 6.25 % = 62.69, rounded to 63, so they owe
    2946. The claim's tax inside is 2946 − 2883 = 63, so D11's difference
    is 0.
    - QuickBooks, billed: `-CR`: Dr labour 18.80, parts 10.03, tax 0.63;
      Cr A/R (plan) 29.46. Then the shortfall invoice: Dr A/R (customer)
      29.46; Cr 18.80, 10.03, 0.63.
    - Absorbed: Dr write-offs 29.46; Cr A/R (plan) 29.46.
    - Xero credit note: −18.80, and −10.66 with expected tax 0.63.
      Absorbed: −29.46, expected tax 0.00.
  - **What 376's code would bill today, which 4A replaces:**
    - on a denial, the gross 23000 plus tax, so the deductible's 2500 a
      second time;
    - on a part approval, 23000 × 2946 / 20946, about 3235 before tax
      against a true 2883.
  - A test holds the customer's total across both invoices, on every
    path, to no more than the repair without a warranty.
- **4B: on a denial, void the deductible and re-bill the whole repair.**
  The customer's original invoice would be voided and replaced, and an
  invoice already paid would be refunded. That is more moving parts than
  the operator's 2a design (the claim runs separately, and a settlement
  bills only the shortfall). Not recommended.

## S0-5: decisions taken here, not stops

- **D1. The CLI:** `--deductible-cents` on `warranty add` and `update`,
  the same unit as `--approved-cents`. `warranty list` shows it as
  dollars, or "not recorded".
- **D2. The packet** shows the warranty's deductible under Coverage. It
  shows the deductible applied under the claim: "Deductible (charged to
  the customer): $25.54, of which tax $0.54", with the covered work and
  the claim net of it. Before the invoice, it says the deductible is
  applied when the invoice is generated.
- **D3. A regenerated invoice keeps its deductible.** It is re-derived
  from the warranty. A claim past draft whose amount would change is
  refused by the existing check (S0-2 item 4), so a changed deductible
  cannot silently move a submitted claim.
- **D4. The deductible's lines sit after the uncovered lines**, and the
  invoice's note names the claim.
- **D5. A migration (084) of new columns and a new table only:**
  - `warranties.deductible_cents`;
  - `warranty_claims.deductible_cents`;
  - `warranty_claim_lines.deductible_cents`;
  - `tax_deductible_rules` with its three US-MA rows.

  It goes through the deploy skill. Live holds 0 warranties and claims,
  so no existing row changes.
- **D6. Command line only.** No API model moves, so there is no mobile
  session.
- **D7. Gate 16's warranty records `--deductible-cents 0`**, so its
  figures do not move. A new test carries the deductible.
