## Risks and stops

Rule 1 stops, which only the operator can clear:
- Login or credential: the Sentry account and its DSN are an outside account the operator opens (the mission's own words). The builder can write the SDK so that it is inert without a DSN, and test it that way. Nothing can be sent live until the operator opens the account and places the DSN per the K62 pattern. The builder must not create the account or handle the key in a chat or the repository.
- A real fork: whether session replay ships at all, and how it is masked. Replay records what appears on a mechanic's screen, and work orders carry customer names and vehicle owners. The options are to ship without replay, to ship with masking, or to ship with no masking. The operator picks. The same data can reach crash reports through breadcrumbs and local variables, so the scrubbing rule is also the operator's to define.
- A threshold: the trace and replay sample rates are thresholds. The builder may propose values, and the operator sets them.
- A launch record: docs/launch-checklist.md's privacy answers and its Privacy Policy URL statement become wrong once Sentry is added. The builder should flag that the checklist needs revision and leave the wording to the operator.
- Outward-facing: any real event sent to Sentry publishes data outside the company. A test event needs the operator's confirmation first.

Not stops: no live-database change or migration is expected.

Note, not a stop: the 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-25 entry's advisor recommendation (not yet confirmed by the operator) would take the staff-login phases (303 and 348) before the shop phases. The "Next, in order" list governs under K48 until the operator changes it. The 2026-10-31, later entry (K63) places Phase 397 between Phase 345 and Phase 343, so the order is settled for now.
