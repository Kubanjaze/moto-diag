## S0-3. The failures, reproduced on demand

A scratch script froze the clock with 370's `frozen_datetime` under
`TZ=America/New_York` (intake_repo, work_order_repo, analytics,
timestamps):

- **F191 in the evening,** 2026-10-06 23:26:39 EDT: `list_intakes(since="30m")`
  listed `[1, 2]`, where 1 was taken three hours earlier. Expected `[2]`.
- **F191 at midday,** 2026-10-15 12:00 EDT: `since="30m"` listed `[]`. The
  intake taken a minute earlier is left out, because on the same date the
  space sorts before the `T`.
- **F186, a window's first day:** two work orders completed on 2026-09-15,
  at 09:00 and 13:00 EDT. `throughput(since="30d")` at 2026-10-15 12:00 EDT
  gave `completed_total=2`. The cutoff is 12:00 EDT on 09-15, so the
  expected total is 1.

So the clock test has three known-red cases before the fix.

(81 lines; 20 are model-year ranges and 6 are comments), for `julianday(`,

**Wrong, and in F186/F191's tables:**
