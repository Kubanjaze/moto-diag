Nothing in the excerpts shows a trigger or any other enforcement of append-only behaviour in the existing audit tables.

## What the row promises

Row 348 promises three things. (1) "Immutable event log": a writer that records events that cannot be altered afterwards. Nothing reaches it today, and the excerpts show no enforcement of immutability, so this needs a trigger or an equivalent check built and tested. (2) "Security audit trail": a way to read who did what and when. No command, API route or screen reads such a trail today; the nearest is the Phase 10 log file, which a user reaches only by reading the log. (3) "Compliance readiness": no user-facing verb is named; it reads as a property of (1) and (2) rather than a separate action, and the row should say what a compliance reader would do. Actor identity for (1) depends on the staff logins the plan puts in Phase 303, which the plan of record orders before 348.
