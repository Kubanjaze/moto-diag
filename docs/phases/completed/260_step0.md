## S0-1 — the plan's nouns in `src/` (and tests): extension, and no new front door is needed

Vocabulary: `pre-purchase`, `PPI`, `frame straight`, `fork seal`,
`steering head`, `swingarm`, `wheel bearing`, `brake`, `tire`, `accident`
— over `src/` and `tests/`, case-insensitive.

- **The substrate now has a front door** (259's build): `cli/workflow.py`
  registers `motodiag workflow list/show` from `cli/main.py`, and
  `template_repo`'s accessors read the tables. A new template seeded by a
  migration is rendered by the existing command with **no new code** — the
  door 259 built is the door this phase walks through.
- **No chassis-PPI content exists beyond `generic_ppi_v1`'s starter
  items** (migration 007): item 2 "Frame inspection" (frame cracks, welds,
  straightness, "check neck bearings for play") and item 5 "Brake and tire
  condition" (pad thickness, rotor thickness, tread depth, DOT date). Both
  are one-line stubs; item 5 carries uncited figures (see the F158-adjacent
  note at the end of this file).
- **Adjacent, not competing:** `advanced/wear_patterns.json` (Phase 128's
  data) has symptom-driven wear patterns for fork seals, steering head
  bearings, swingarm pivot bearings and rear wheel bearings — a diagnostic
  model keyed on rider-reported symptoms, not an inspection protocol; and
  rows 232/358/611 of `advanced/data/tsbs.json` are TSB records that cite
  chassis subjects. Neither is checklist content and neither is
  user-reachable as a protocol; no conflict.
- **Mobile: zero hits** for `workflow template|pre-purchase|ppi_` across
  the mobile repo's source — the substrate has no mobile surface, and this
  phase does not add one.

**Decision: extension, no reshape, no new module.** One content migration
(068) plus tests. The `WorkflowCategory.PPI` enumeration already exists;
`list_templates`/`get_template_by_slug`/`get_checklist_items` already
render; the CLI group already reaches them.

## S0-3 — the evidence census: what the library supports per subject

Method (the F152/259 lesson, and this session's instruction 6): every
`*.pdf` under `~/research/motodiag` minus the two venvs — **260 files:
157 read fully (text on every page), 73 partially (text on some pages —
searched over the pages they yield), and 30 yield no text at all** (16
will not open — F127's download debris — plus 14 image-only documents,
F127(a)'s OCR-needed class: `grom_service.pdf`,
`piaggio_primavera_om.pdf`, the budget-scooter manuals and the like;
`parse_counts.py` in the session tmp holds the list);
**every search strips all whitespace from each page's text before
matching**, so letter-spaced OCR cannot hide a hit. Every negative below
states its vocabulary, its scope, its count, and a control that proves
the search can see the pages in question.

## S0-4 — the powertrain boundary

Chassis subjects are **not** ICE subjects: an electric motorcycle has a
frame, forks, steering-head bearings, a rear arm, wheel bearings, disc
brakes and tires — every item in this protocol applies. So the template
sets `applicable_powertrains` to **all three** (`["ice","electric",
"hybrid"]`, `generic_ppi_v1`'s own value) rather than 259's ICE-restricted
`["ice","hybrid"]`. The items' wording stays honest for electric machines
(hydraulic brakes, forks and tires are powertrain-agnostic; no item
presumes an engine).

## Refuted after the fact (the Opus refute pass, 2026-09-25)

The swingarm negative above does not hold. Piaggio's manuals call the
part a "swinging arm", which `swing[- ]?arm` cannot match on
whitespace-stripped text ("swingingarm"). The Vespa GTS Super 300 ie
(2008) service station manual (`pdfs/vespa_gts300_shop.pdf`, PDF p. 227)
sets the axial clearance between its two swinging arms at "Standard
clearance 0.40 ÷ 0.60 mm Allowable limit after use: 1.5 mm". The frame
and wheel-bearing negatives survive the widened vocabulary. The rest is
in the phase log's Refuter pass.

The measurement above is left as it was taken. It is the record of what
a vocabulary without the maker's word finds.
