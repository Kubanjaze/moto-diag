### 2026-09-08 — Paired research, close

- **MV Agusta had zero entries and has no adapter rows**; the tooling gap
  stays guarded for row 235. Unlike the Aprilia pair, the ground here is
  genuinely uncovered: `counter-rotating` returned one file corpus-wide
  and `radial valve` zero.
- **Research was paired with Phase 234** in one capped 6-agent run — the
  second pairing in this block.
- **Both refuters caught the same error, and it was dangerous.** The
  research called the Dragster loose-spoke recall UK-only and advised
  checking spoke tension. Both found it in the US database, flagged
  **do-not-ride**, with the published remedy being **replacement of the
  rear wheel** — the nipples' surface treatment is out of specification,
  so the torque cannot hold and re-tensioning does not restore them.
  Following the original guidance would have left a defective wheel on a
  do-not-ride machine. The entry now leads with that.
- **A second correction on the same entry**: the swingarm pin bolt fails
  **in service** from incorrect heat treatment, not while being
  tightened. The distinction matters because a correctly torqued bolt is
  then not evidence of safety.
- **The counter-rotation entry declines to state the direction.** The
  counter-rotation itself is well sourced and the consequence is real —
  "turn it in the direction of rotation" means the opposite of the
  reflex, and turning it wrong can slacken the cam chain through the
  one-way tensioner. But MV's stated direction could not be opened from
  any manufacturer document, so the entry sends the mechanic to the
  machine's timing marks. A test asserts no direction word appears.
- **The starter-clutch entry is marked `forum`** because it rests on
  owner reports: the unit is handed for the reverse rotation, failure is
  the most-discussed complaint, a weak battery is repeatedly named as the
  cause, and it is reported to have been reassembled backwards during
  repair — reproducing the symptom it was fitted to cure.
- **A scope entry on national versus global recall populations**: the
  manufacturer's own bulletin names far more machines and models than the
  national campaign, so a clean national check on an imported bike
  answers a different question from the one being asked. Neither document
  is wrong; they scope differently.
- **Intervals are deferred and the widely quoted F3 figure is not
  repeated**, because it could not be confirmed against MV's own table.
  Clearance specification values are omitted entirely — the sources
  consulted disagreed and none was authoritative.
- **Thirteenth mention-versus-use slip**, plus a second of the same
  family: a check matched the sentence *denying* the wrong failure mode,
  and another matched a figure inside a **symptom** — the mechanic's own
  words rather than a claim. Both now run on assertion-bearing fields
  with negation awareness.
- 837 → 842; 30 phase tests; regression 5653/0; F9 clean.
- **Key finding: the obvious repair can be the wrong one, and only the
  campaign's own mechanism tells you.**
