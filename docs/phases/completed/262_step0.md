# Phase 262 — Step 0: what the tree, the library, the regulators and the live database hold before the plan

**Measured 2026-09-26, before v1.0.** Phase 262 carries Track N batch 3:
ROADMAP rows 262 (crash and insurance claim support), 263 (track-day and
race prep) and 267 (emissions and smog compliance, California first),
plus three fixes in the same migration: F161, F162 and W30. One Step 0
covers all of it. Scripts are in the session scratchpad (`s0/`). Page
citations are PDF pages (the F152 convention). Regulator web pages have
no pages; they are cited by document and section.

## S0-1: the plan's nouns in `src/` and mobile. An extension, with no new front door

Searched over `src/` and `../moto-diag-mobile/src`: `crash`,
`insurance`, `salvage`, `track.day`, `race prep`, `lockwire`,
`safety.wire`, `emission`, `smog`, `CARB`, `tech inspection`,
`regulation`, `provenance`.

- **The three categories already exist.** `WorkflowCategory` in
  `workflows/models.py` has had `CRASH_SUPPORT`, `TRACK_PREP` and
  `EMISSIONS` since Phase 114. None has a template.
- **The front door exists.** `motodiag workflow list/show` renders any
  seeded template, and `--category` is built from the enum.
- **Adjacent, not competing.** The `crash` (56 files), `emission` (56)
  and `CARB` (89, almost all "carb" for carburetor) hits are
  `known_issues` seed rows, recall data, the report renderer and
  diagnostic text. None is a procedure reachable as a workflow. Mobile:
  `crash` (13) and `insurance` (3) are app-crash handling and a billing
  string; the rest are 0.
- **Provenance `regulation` exists only on `known_issues.source`**
  (migrations 3585 on, `cli/kb.py` `VERIFIED_SOURCES`). `checklist_items`
  has no source column. The operator chose to name the law in the text
  and record `regulation` per claim in the claims table, with no schema
  change (S0-4).

**Decision: an extension with no reshape and no new module,** as in
259–264: one content migration (071) seeding three templates, the three
fixes, and tests.

## S0-8: acquiring the regulators' text

Acquisition in this project is a script, never a model (F141). The
batch's regulator documents were fetched by `acquire.py`'s own `Fetcher`
and `save()`, from `s0/acquire262.py`. Robots.txt is honoured, one
request a second, and each original lands in
`~/research/motodiag/acquired/Regulation/` with a sidecar the script
writes (url, status, bytes, sha256, fetched time) and a derived text.

- **Not fetched, by robots.txt:** leginfo.legislature.ca.gov
  (`User-agent: * / Disallow: /`) and the eCFR API. Two leginfo pages
  (H&S §44011, VC §27156) were opened by hand with `curl` before the
  robots rule was read. Nothing from them is saved or cited.
- **Fetched, 12 of 13:**
  - BAR, Smog Check Reference Guide (PDF, 50 pages);
  - CARB: Aftermarket Motorcycle Parts; Motorcycle Aftermarket Parts;
    Highway Motorcycle Emissions Certification Requirements; ONMC
    Executive Order Introduction; California Racing Vehicles —
    Aftermarket Parts and Executive Orders;
  - California DMV, Vehicle Industry Registration Procedures Manual:
    19.015 Definitions, 19.075 Salvage Certificate, 19.045 Notice of
    Retention by Owner, 19.040 Nonrepairable Vehicle Certificate; and
    the DMV page Total Loss Salvage and Non-Repairable Vehicles;
  - EPA, fact sheet on aftermarket defeat devices and tampering (PDF).
- **Refused, recorded and not retried:** EPA's 2020 enforcement alert
  (HTTP 403).
- **A second run (`s0/acquire262b.py`), one page:** the DMV's "Junk/Revived
  Salvage Vehicles", which the total-loss page links for the repair path,
  with that page recorded as its referrer.

The pages are merged into the census as `s0/pages_reg.json`. A PDF is
split into pages; an HTML page is one page.
