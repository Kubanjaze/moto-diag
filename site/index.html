What Mekno does

Hears the problem

Record a cold start, a rattle or a misfire on your phone. Mekno compares it with how that engine should sound.

Knows the bike

Known issues, common failures and fixes by make, model and year: Harley-Davidson, Honda, Yamaha, Kawasaki, Suzuki and more.

Reads the codes

Look up a fault code and see what it means on that bike, and what to rule out first.

Keeps the history

Every bike's service history, upcoming maintenance and job notes in one place for the whole shop.

Built for working shops

Mekno is made for mechanics: quick in the bay, clear for the customer, and honest about what it is sure of and what still needs checking. Its suggestions help a skilled technician decide faster. The technician always makes the call.
