What Mekno does

Hears the problem

Record the bike on your phone. Mekno reads the symptoms out of your voice note and looks at the video with you.

Knows the bike

Known issues, common failures and fixes by make, model and year: Harley-Davidson, Honda, Yamaha, Kawasaki, Suzuki and more.

Reads the codes

Look up a fault code and see what it means on that bike, and what to rule out first.

Keeps the history

Every bike's service history, upcoming maintenance and job notes in one place for the whole shop.

Built for working shops

Mekno is made for mechanics: quick in the bay, clear for the customer, and honest about what it is sure of and what still needs checking. Its suggestions help a skilled technician decide faster. The technician always makes the call.

Questions shops ask

How accurate is it?

We haven't measured that yet, so we don't print a number. We will once we have one we can stand behind.

What Mekno does show is how sure it is: each diagnosis comes with a confidence and a severity.

It can be wrong. An AI model can give a confident answer that is false, and a known issue can miss the bike in front of you. Check before you act.

Who makes the call?

Your tech does. Mekno's suggestions are information to help a mechanic, not instructions. The mechanic decides what to do to the bike, and the shop is responsible for the repair. Mekno helps a skilled tech decide faster; it doesn't decide for them.

Does it work with our shop software?

Mekno sits beside it. You can use Mekno for the diagnosis and keep your own system for everything else. Mekno can also keep the bikes, work orders and invoices, if you want them in one place. It doesn't connect to other shop software today.

Who owns our data?

The shop's data is the shop's. "What a shop puts into Mekno is the shop's, its customers' details included. We do not own it and we do not sell it. We use it only to run the service for the shop, as the privacy policy says." (Terms)

"We do not sell anyone's data, and we do not use it for advertising." (Privacy)

Taking it out: "A shop that leaves can take its data out, and a shop can ask for it to be deleted". "We send a copy in a format other software can read, and we tell the shop when the deletion is done." (Privacy) Ask through the support page.

Backups: "The encrypted nightly backups are kept with no end date, and are opened only to restore the service after a failure. A shop's data deleted from the service stays in the encrypted copies made before the deletion." (Privacy)

The full list of services your data goes to, and why, is in the privacy policy.
