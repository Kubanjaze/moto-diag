What Mekno is

Mekno is a tool for motorcycle repair shops and riders. A shop keeps its staff, its customers, their bikes, work orders, invoices, diagnoses, photos, videos and voice notes in it. Mekno suggests what may be wrong with a bike, from its knowledge base and from an AI model, and works out figures such as labour, parts and tax on an invoice. A subscription is paid through Stripe.
